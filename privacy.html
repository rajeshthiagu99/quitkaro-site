QuitKaro privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026. This policy covers QuitKaro on Android (Google Play) and on iPhone (App Store).

What stays on your phone

Your answers stay on your phone: how many cigarettes you smoke and which brand, what one costs, your reasons, when cravings hit, the cigarettes you log, your quit start time and your daily check-in. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. The only things that leave your phone for your account are listed under "Your account and friends" below. If phone backup is on, Google (Android) or Apple (iCloud) may back up the app's data to your own account.

What is sent, and to whom

To understand how the app is used and to measure our advertising, the app sends usage events. An event is a fixed name such as "onboarding_completed", "paywall_viewed" or "checkout_started", plus, for a purchase, the price, currency and product, and, for sign-in, only whether you used Google or Apple. Each install has a random app ID that is sent with these events. Cravings, slips, cigarette counts, your day count, your name and your answers are never sent to these services.

  • Mixpanel (product analytics), on Android and iPhone.
  • Meta (Facebook) app events, on Android and iPhone, to measure whether our ads on Facebook and Instagram bring people to the app.
  • Google Firebase Analytics and RevenueCat (subscription status), on Android only.

These services also receive device information they collect themselves, such as app version, device model, operating system and country. Because this is a quit-smoking app, an event by itself tells these companies that you use one.

On iPhone, the app shows what it sends before anything is sent, and then Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Only if you choose Allow are your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and the random app ID shared with Meta; if you choose Ask App Not to Track, Meta receives the events without them. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

On Android, these services may receive the Android advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Your account and friends

To use QuitKaro you sign in with Google (Android and iPhone) or Apple (iPhone). Google or Apple tells us a random account ID and your first name. We keep the ID, not your email address.

Our own server (in Germany, run by us) stores only: which of Google or Apple you used and that random ID, your display name (prefilled from your first name or what you typed; you can change it), a 6-character friend code, your current and best smoke-free streak in days, who you are friends with, and when these were created or changed. It does not store your email, your answers, your cigarette counts or your logs. You stay signed in with a random session token.

Your friends see your display name and streak. The leaderboard shows display names and streaks to other QuitKaro users. Everything is sent over an encrypted connection and is not shared with or sold to anyone.

Invite links are short links on our server that carry your friend code. On Android, after someone installs from your link, the app reads the code from Google Play (Install Referrer) so you become friends. On iPhone, the app reads a code from the clipboard only when you tap Paste.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play on Android and by Apple on iPhone. We do not see your payment details. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in Google Play or in your iPhone's Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

Health information

QuitKaro shows general information about quitting smoking, with sources (World Health Organization, NHS). It is not medical advice and does not replace a doctor or a stop-smoking service.

Deleting your data

To delete your account, go to Settings > Delete account in the app. This deletes your account, display name, friend code, streaks, friendships and sessions from our server straight away. You can also ask by email (below) from the address you contact us with, giving your display name and friend code. Delete the app (or clear its storage on Android) to delete what is on your phone. Analytics events are kept by Mixpanel and Meta under their own retention rules and are linked only to the random app ID; to ask for them to be deleted, email swedhabhaga1612@gmail.com with the Feedback ID from the app's "Need help?" message, or the date you installed the app.

Children

QuitKaro is meant for adults who smoke. It is not directed at children.

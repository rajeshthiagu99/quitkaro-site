QuitKaro privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026. This policy covers QuitKaro on Android (Google Play) and on iPhone (App Store).

What stays on your phone

Your answers stay on your phone: your first name if you give one, how many cigarettes you smoke and which brand, what one costs, your reasons, when cravings hit, the cigarettes you log, your quit start time and your daily check-in. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. There is no QuitKaro account and no sign-up. If phone backup is on, Google (Android) or Apple (iCloud) may back up the app's data to your own account.

What is sent, and to whom

To understand how the app is used and to measure our advertising, the app sends usage events. An event is a fixed name such as "onboarding_completed", "paywall_viewed" or "checkout_started", plus, for a purchase, the price, currency and product. Each install has a random app ID that is sent with these events. Cravings, slips, cigarette counts, your day count, your name and your answers are never sent.

  • Mixpanel (product analytics), on Android and iPhone.
  • Meta (Facebook) app events, on Android and iPhone, to measure whether our ads on Facebook and Instagram bring people to the app.
  • Google Firebase Analytics and RevenueCat (subscription status), on Android only.

These services also receive device information they collect themselves, such as app version, device model, operating system and country. Because this is a quit-smoking app, an event by itself tells these companies that you use one.

On iPhone, the app shows what it sends before anything is sent, and then Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Only if you choose Allow are your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and the random app ID shared with Meta; if you choose Ask App Not to Track, Meta receives the events without them. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

On Android, these services may receive the Android advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Friends (optional)

If you choose to use Friends and get a friend code, your first name, your friend code, and how many cigarettes you logged each day are stored in Google Firebase under an anonymous ID, so the friends you share your code with can see them. Nothing else leaves your phone. Friends is not available in every version of the app.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play on Android and by Apple on iPhone. We do not see your payment details. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in Google Play or in your iPhone's Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

Health information

QuitKaro shows general information about quitting smoking, with sources (World Health Organization, NHS). It is not medical advice and does not replace a doctor or a stop-smoking service.

Deleting your data

Delete the app (or clear its storage on Android) to delete what is on your phone. Analytics events are kept by Mixpanel and Meta under their own retention rules and are linked only to the random app ID; to ask for them to be deleted, contact us with the Feedback ID from the app's "Need help?" message, or the date you installed the app.

Children

QuitKaro is meant for adults who smoke. It is not directed at children.

Contact

Use the developer email address shown on the QuitKaro page on Google Play or the App Store.

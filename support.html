QuitKaro support

Help for QuitKaro on iPhone and Android.

Contact

Email swedhabhaga1612@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. Please say whether you use iPhone or Android.

Restore QuitKaro Plus

Open any locked item (for example the full health timeline on the Progress tab) and tap Restore purchases on the QuitKaro Plus screen. On iPhone, use the Apple Account you paid with; on Android, the Google account you paid with.

Cancel or change your subscription

On iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > QuitKaro Plus. On Android: Play Store > your profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Reminders are not showing

Check that notifications are allowed for QuitKaro in your phone's Settings. The daily check-in is free; the hard-hour nudges are part of QuitKaro Plus.

Add the widget

On iPhone: touch and hold the home screen > Edit > Add Widget > QuitKaro. On Android: touch and hold the home screen > Widgets > QuitKaro.

Your data

Your answers and logs stay on your phone. See the privacy policy for what is sent and how to have it deleted.

Health

QuitKaro gives general information, not medical advice. For help with quitting or your health, talk to a doctor or a stop-smoking service.

QuitKaro privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

What stays on your phone

Your answers stay on your phone: your first name if you give one, how many cigarettes you smoke, what one costs, your reasons, when cravings hit, your quit start time and your daily check-in. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. If Android backup is on, Google may back up the app's data to your own Google account.

What is sent

To understand how the app is used and to measure advertising, the app sends usage events to:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Mixpanel
  • Meta (Facebook) app events
  • RevenueCat (subscription status)

An event is a fixed name such as "onboarding_completed", "paywall_viewed" or "craving_started", plus, for a purchase, the price and product. These services also receive device information they collect themselves, including the Android advertising ID, app version, device model and country. Because this is a quit-smoking app, an event by itself tells these companies that you use one.

We do not send your answers, your name or anything you type.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to delete what is on your phone.

Contact: use the developer email address shown on the QuitKaro page on Google Play.
